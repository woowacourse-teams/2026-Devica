## Brand & Style

This design system is built for high-stakes professional environments where clarity and speed of information processing are paramount. The aesthetic is rooted in **Industrial Minimalism**, drawing inspiration from precision engineering tools and technical documentation. It avoids all decorative flourishes to focus entirely on utility and the hierarchy of data.

The target audience consists of specialists who require an uncluttered, high-performance interface that feels like a physical piece of laboratory or industrial equipment. The emotional response is one of serious reliability, absolute precision, and technical authority.

## Colors

The palette is strictly grayscale to eliminate visual noise and ensure that any status-based colors (like red for errors) are immediately impactful. 

**The frontmatter is the source of truth.** The values below are corrected to match it.

- **Primary:** Pure black `#000000` for high-contrast text, primary actions, and structural borders.
- **Body text:** `#1a1c1c` (`on-surface`). Supporting copy uses `#4c4546` (`on-surface-variant`).
- **Disabled / placeholder:** `#7e7576` (`outline`). Hairline dividers use `#cfc4c5` (`outline-variant`).
- **Background:** `#f9f9f9` (`surface`). Cards and panels sit on pure white `#ffffff` (`surface-container-lowest`).
- **Error:** `#ba1a1a` — the only chromatic color in the system.

Interactive states use value shifts (lightening or darkening the gray) rather than hue changes to maintain the industrial rigor.

## Typography

The system utilizes **Inter** for its systematic, utilitarian legibility in high-density layouts. For technical metadata and UI labels, **Geist** provides a monospaced, developer-friendly feel that reinforces the precision-tool aesthetic.

**Korean text uses Pretendard.** Inter and Geist are Latin-only typefaces with no Hangul glyphs. Without an explicit Korean face, Hangul falls back to whatever the device happens to provide, and its weight and spacing drift away from the Latin text. Pretendard is built on Inter, so the Latin portions line up almost exactly.

Labels are all-caps Latin (`PROCESSOR`, `MEMORY`, `STORAGE`, `OS`) and use Geist. **When the label's content is Korean — a `준비 중` badge, button text — keep the label size and letter-spacing but switch the family to Pretendard.** Size tokens and font families are decided separately.

Hierarchy is established through weight and capitalization rather than color. On mobile devices, "Display" and "Headline-LG" scale down by 20% to maintain readability while preserving the tight, technical spacing.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a **Rigid 4px Grid** to ensure all elements align with mathematical precision.

**Mobile-first, single column. This product has no sidebar.** Content is centered within a max width and breathes through wider gutters as the viewport grows — the layout structure itself stays identical at every size.

- Container: `max-width: 1120px`, centered
- Horizontal margin: **16px** on mobile, **32px** from 768px up
- **One breakpoint only: 768px**
- Card lists go 1 column → 2 columns at 768px. Nothing else changes.
- Avoid fixed pixel widths. Use `max-width` with `%` or `fr`.

Margins are kept tight to maximize information density. Use `density-high` padding for rows in dense views. Gutters are strictly enforced at 16px. All containers are top-aligned.

## Elevation & Depth

This system rejects shadows in favor of **Tonal Layering and Bold Outlines**. Depth is communicated through value contrast:
- **Level 0 (Base):** `#f9f9f9` background.
- **Level 1 (Surfaces):** Pure white `#ffffff` panels with a 1px `#1a1c1c` border.
- **Level 2 (Popovers/Modals):** Pure white panels with a 2px solid black border and a hard, 0-offset "block shadow" (a solid 4px black offset with 0 blur).

Separation of concerns is achieved through hair-line dividers (1px) and subtle background shifts rather than atmospheric lighting.

## Shapes

The shape language is **strictly orthogonal**. All corners are 0px (sharp). This reinforces the industrial, high-precision aesthetic and ensures that elements can be packed tightly together without the wasted white space inherent in rounded corners. Every button, input field, card, badge, and modal must adhere to this 90-degree geometry.

**The single exception is the circular loading indicator.** A spinner reads as rotation only when round.

## Components

### Buttons
Buttons are rectangular with 1px solid borders. 
- **Primary:** Black background with white text.
- **Secondary:** White background with black border and black text.
- **Tertiary:** No border, text-only with underline on hover.
- **Interaction:** On hover, primary buttons invert (white background, black text).

### Input Fields
Inputs use a 1px solid gray border that thickens to 2px black on focus. Labels sit above the field in the `label-sm` style. Error states are indicated by a 2px red border, the only use of color in the system.

### Spec Rows
**This product does not use data tables** — horizontal scrolling breaks on mobile. Technical specs are shown as **label-value rows stacked vertically**: an all-caps `label-md` label above its value in `body-md`. Separate rows with 1px hairline dividers (`outline-variant`).

The same spec row appears on the recommendation, confirmation, product list, guide, and product detail screens. It must look identical in all of them.

### States
Since the palette is grayscale, states are expressed through inversion and shape rather than hue.

- **Selected / active:** black fill, white text
- **Default:** white surface, 1px border
- **Disabled / coming soon:** gray text plus an explicit badge. Graying alone does not tell the user why it is not clickable.
- **Emphasis:** size and weight, never color

### Chips/Tags
Rectangular boxes with a 1px border. No rounded corners. Used primarily for status or category indicators.

### Cards
Cards are simple containers with 1px borders. No shadows. They are used to group related technical parameters or data visualizations.

### Segmented Controls
Used for switching views. Elements are placed side-by-side with shared borders, creating a unified "switch" look. The active state is indicated by a solid black fill.
